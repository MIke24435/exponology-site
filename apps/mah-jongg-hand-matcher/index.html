How to read a hand card

Help

This is the real top result for the rack above. Six things on it — numbered here, explained at the side.

1

Hand ID + ★Find E2.2 on your printed card, in Section E — Consecutive Run. The gold star means the hand has singles or pairs — here the 3-4-5 run — and a Joker can’t fill those. The X · Exposed badge means the hand may be exposed.

2

PointsThe hand’s value, as printed on the card — 25 pts here.

3

The three circlesBrown 12 Have (tiles you hold toward this hand, not counting Jokers) + purple 1 Jokers used + red 1 Don’t Have. They always add up to 14.

4

The red note“Needs a 5 you don’t hold.” The 5 of Craks is a single in the run, so no Joker can cover it — you must draw it. Often the decider between two close hands.

5

The lined-up handEvery tile in the hand, in its groups — number over suit letter (B, C, D) — marked against your rack: X you hold it, J a Joker fills it (the fourth 6 of Bams), blank you still need it (the 5 of Craks).

6

Below the lineYour tiles that don’t help this hand — just the 9 of Dots here. Short row = almost everything you hold is working. Long row = a lot set aside.

How to use it — four taps

Help
1

Add tiles

Tap a kind — Bams, Craks, Dots, Winds, Dragons — then the tile. Flower and Joker go straight in, one tap.

2

Fix mistakes

Tap any tile in Your Rack to take it back out.

3

Arrange / Clear

Arrange sorts the rack neatly. Clear empties it.

4

Random hand

Top right of Add tiles. Deals a practice hand — 14 tiles with East checked, 13 without.

Narrowing the results

Sort all checked ranks everything in one list — all 79 hands here. Unchecked, hands missing a single or pair drop into a separate group below, so the Joker-friendly hands stay on top.
